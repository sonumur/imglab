How to Calculate CGPA to Percentage

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is the overall measure of a student's academic performance across all semesters. Most Indian universities such as VTU, Anna University, Mumbai University, GTU, and AKTU use a 10-point CGPA grading scale.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours
Where Σ means "sum of all subjects"

CGPA to Percentage Formula (10-Point Scale)

The most widely used formula in India, adopted by VTU, Anna University, and many other universities:

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
Example: 8.2 CGPA × 9.5 = 77.9%

Step-by-Step Example

Suppose you have the following subjects in Semester 1:

Subject Grade Points Credits Weighted Points
Mathematics 9 4 36
Physics 8 3 24
Programming 8 4 32
Electronics 7 3 21
Workshop 9 2 18
Total 16 131

CGPA = 131 ÷ 16 = 8.19  →  Percentage = 8.19 × 9.5 = 77.8%

University-Specific Formulas

  • VTU: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • Anna University: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • Mumbai University: Percentage = (CGPA – 0.5) × 10
  • GTU: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • AKTU: Percentage = CGPA × 10
  • SPPU / Pune University: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
Frequently Asked Questions
How to convert 8.5 CGPA to percentage?+
On a 10-point scale (VTU, Anna University etc.): 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%. For Mumbai University: (8.5 – 0.5) × 10 = 80%.
What is 7.5 CGPA in percentage?+
7.5 CGPA × 9.5 = 71.25% on a 10-point scale. This falls under the First Class category in most Indian universities.
Is 7 CGPA good for placements?+
Yes, a CGPA of 7.0 (equivalent to ~66.5%) meets the minimum shortlist criteria for most Indian IT companies and government PSUs. Many MNCs prefer 7.5+ for campus drives.
What is 10 CGPA in percentage?+
10.0 CGPA on a 10-point scale equals 95% – 100%. It represents the highest academic achievement in a 10-point grading system.
What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?+
GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated for a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the overall average across all semesters of your degree programme, weighted by credit hours.
How to convert CGPA to percentage in Mumbai University?+
Mumbai University uses the formula: Percentage = (CGPA – 0.5) × 10. For example, a CGPA of 7.5 = (7.5 – 0.5) × 10 = 70%.

About CGPA to Percentage Conversion

How is CGPA Converted to Percentage?

Different Indian universities follow different official conversion formulas. The most commonly used formula across India is Percentage = CGPA × 9.5, which is approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education).

Why Multiply by 9.5?

This multiplier comes from calibrating the mid-point of a grade range. For example, a student with a Grade Point of 10 is expected to have scored between 91% and 100%, whose midpoint is approximately 95. Dividing 95 by 10 gives the multiplier of 9.5.

The AICTE-recommended formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 is used by VTU, Anna University, GTU, SPPU, and most autonomous engineering colleges across India.

What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is the grade point average for a single semester, calculated based on that semester's subjects and credit hours. It is different from CGPA, which is the cumulative average across all semesters.

How to Calculate CGPA from SGPA?

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Total Credits

This is a weighted average — semesters with more credit hours have a greater impact on your final CGPA. Simply adding SGPAs and dividing by the number of semesters gives a wrong answer unless all semesters have equal credits.

Example Calculation

Semester SGPA Credits SGPA × Credits
Semester 1 8.2 22 180.4
Semester 2 7.8 24 187.2
Semester 3 8.5 24 204.0
Semester 4 8.0 22 176.0
Total 92 747.6

CGPA = 747.6 ÷ 92 = 8.13
