About ImgLab Tools

ImgLab Tools (powered by ImgLab Tools) is dedicated to helping Indian students navigate the complex world of academic grade conversions. Different universities use different formulas to convert CGPA to percentage, and vice-versa. Our mission is to provide a single, reliable platform where students can find accurate, university-specific calculators for all their needs.

What we offer

  • CGPA to Percentage: Support for VTU, Anna University, Mumbai University, AKTU, and more.
  • SGPA to CGPA: Weighted average calculation based on semi-semester credits.
  • Grade Calculator: Subject-wise marks to grade point conversion.
  • Reference Tables: Quick look-up charts for 10-point and 4-point scales.

Our Commitment

We strive for 100% accuracy in our formulas by cross-referencing official university circulars. However, results provided by our tools are for informational purposes only. Always consult your official university transcript for certified records.
